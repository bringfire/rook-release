# Rook Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: June 1, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Bringfire Games, LLC ("Bringfire Games", "we",
"us", or "our") handles information in connection with Rook — the Rook plug-ins for
Rhino 3D and Grasshopper, the Rook MCP server, and the accompanying skills, hooks,
agents, knowledge stores, and tools (the "Software").

**The short version:** Rook runs on your machine. We don't operate servers that
receive your designs, prompts, or results, and we don't require an account. Your AI
requests go directly from your device to the providers you choose with your own API
keys. We don't sell data, and we don't train on your content.

## 1. Our approach

Rook is a desktop plug-in, not a cloud service. It is local-first and
bring-your-own-key (BYOK). As a result, we collect little to no personal
information, and your design content never passes through our systems.

## 2. Information we do not collect

Through normal use of the Software, we do not collect:

- your Rhino or Grasshopper files, geometry, or models;
- your prompts, instructions, or other Inputs;
- the geometry, images, meshes, text, or other Outputs the Software produces;
- your API keys or provider credentials; or
- account information — because the Software does not require an account with us.

## 3. Your content stays with you

The content you provide to the Software ("Inputs") and the results it produces
("Outputs") are processed locally on your device. When you use an AI feature, the
request is sent directly from your device to the third-party provider you have
configured (for example, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Ollama, LM Studio, fal.ai,
Replicate, or Tencent). We do not operate servers that receive, relay, or store
your Inputs or Outputs, and we do not use them to train any model. Generated
artifacts and the knowledge Rook builds up are stored locally on your device.

## 4. Credentials and API keys

To use AI features you supply your own provider credentials and API keys. These are
stored locally on your device and used to communicate directly with the providers
you select. We do not receive or store them. You are responsible for keeping them
secure and for any charges your providers bill to you.

## 5. Third-party providers

When you use an AI provider through Rook, your use of that provider is governed by
that provider's own privacy policy and terms — not this one. We do not control how
third-party providers collect, use, retain, or transfer data. Please review the
policies of any provider you configure. The Software also runs within Rhino 3D and
Grasshopper, software of Robert McNeel & Associates, which is governed by McNeel's
own terms and policies.

## 6. Diagnostic and usage information

The Software may, where applicable, transmit minimal diagnostic or usage
information (such as version or error information) to help us maintain and improve
it. Any such information excludes your Inputs, Outputs, prompts, files, and
credentials. Where this is optional, you may disable it. We do not use diagnostic
information to identify you or to build a profile of you, and we do not sell it.

## 7. This documentation website

The Rook documentation website is published as static pages (for example, via
GitHub Pages or a similar host). We do not add advertising or cross-site tracking
to it. The hosting provider may automatically log standard technical request
information (such as IP address and browser type) as part of serving the site; that
processing is governed by the host's own privacy policy.

## 8. Cookies and analytics

The Software does not use cookies. We do not add third-party advertising or
behavioral-analytics trackers.

## 9. Data retention

Because we do not receive your Inputs, Outputs, files, or credentials, we do not
retain them. Any minimal diagnostic information (Section 6), if collected, is kept
only as long as needed for the purpose described and is then deleted or
de-identified.

## 10. Security

Your Software, content, and credentials live on your device, so their security
depends on your device's security. We design the Software to keep your content and
keys local. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; you are
responsible for safeguarding your device, files, and credentials.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR/UK
GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA — including the right to access, correct, delete, or port your
personal information, or to object to or restrict its processing. Because we hold
little or no personal information about you, in many cases we will have nothing to
act on; where we do, you may contact us using Section 13 and we will respond as
required by applicable law.

## 12. Children

Rook is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please
contact us and we will address it.

## 13. International users

Rook operates locally on your device. Where you choose to use a third-party
provider, that provider may process your data in other countries under its own
terms. Any limited diagnostic information we receive may be processed in the United
States or other countries where we or our service providers operate.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the Effective
Date above and, for material changes, provide reasonable notice. Your continued use
of the Software after an update means you accept the revised policy.
